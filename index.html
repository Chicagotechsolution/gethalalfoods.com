Three rules every vendor agrees to.

Sourcing on the label

Meat listings state the supplier, slaughter method, and certifier. Packaged goods state the certifying body printed on the package. No listing ships without it.

No pay-to-rank

Search results order by relevance and freshness. Vendors can't buy their way to the top of the shelf, and sponsored placement is always labeled if we ever add it.

Cold chain or it doesn't ship

Fresh and frozen orders ship insulated with tracked delivery windows. If a vendor can't meet the cold-chain standard for your zip code, the listing says so up front.

Founding vendors wanted.

We're onboarding a first group of halal butchers, importers, and small food makers before launch. Founding vendors help shape the catalog rules and keep a reduced commission rate for their first two years.

  • You keep your brand — your shop page, your packaging, your story.
  • We handle the storefront, payments, and order routing.
  • Sourcing documentation reviewed once, then shown on every listing.
